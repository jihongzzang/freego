Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 16, 2025

Summary: This app does not collect any personal information. All data is stored only on your device.

1. Information Collection

The Refrigerator Inventory Management App (hereinafter "Freego App") does not collect any personal information.

  • No registration required - No collection of personal identification information such as accounts, emails, or passwords
  • No analytics tools - Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, etc. are not used
  • No third-party services - No integration with advertising, social login, or other external services
  • No network communication - No data is transmitted to servers

2. Data Storage

This app stores the following data only on your device:

  • Ingredient Information: Name, category, storage location, expiration date, quantity, emoji, memo
  • Shopping List: Items to purchase, category, memo
  • App Settings: Notification settings, user customization options
  • Achievement Progress: Usage statistics and achievement records

This data is stored encrypted in the device's local storage (AsyncStorage) and is not transmitted externally.

3. Data Deletion

Users can completely delete their data at any time:

  • Delete individual items within the app
  • All data is permanently deleted from the device when the app is uninstalled
  • Delete app data through device settings

Important: Since data is stored only on the device, recovery is not possible after app deletion. Users must back up data themselves if needed.

4. Notification Feature

This app uses local push notifications only:

  • Expiration date alerts (sent directly from the device at user-set times)
  • No external push notification servers used
  • Notification content is not transmitted externally
  • Users can disable notifications at any time in settings

5. Permission Requests

This app requests only the following permissions:

  • Notification Permission: To send expiration date notifications

These permissions are used only to provide core app functionality and are not used for any other purpose.

6. Data Security

Since all data is stored only on your device, data security depends on your device's security settings (password, biometrics, etc.). Users are responsible for maintaining their device security.

7. Children's Privacy

Since this app does not collect personal information, it can be safely used by users of all ages, including children under 13.

8. Third-Party Information Sharing

This app does not share any information with third parties.

  • No advertising networks used
  • No analytics services used
  • No cloud backup services used
  • No social media integration

9. Privacy Policy Changes

This privacy policy may be updated according to changes in relevant laws and app features. Changes will be announced through in-app notices or update notes, and significant changes will be announced in advance.

10. User Rights

Users have the following rights:

  • The right to view, modify, and delete data within the app at any time
  • The right to inquire about the privacy policy
  • The right to disable notification features
  • The right to stop using the app at any time
